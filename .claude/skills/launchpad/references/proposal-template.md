# Proposal document: canonical structure and boilerplate

Source of truth for new proposals. Last synced from proposal 016-260807 (PRO), whose
legals came from 012-260306 (Recoding America), Joel's most recent signed format. When
Joel updates his legal terms in a newer proposal, update this file.

## Notion block vocabulary

The web parser understands: `heading_1` (major sections), `heading_2` (subsections),
`heading_3` (sub-subsections like milestones), `paragraph`, `bulleted_list_item`,
`numbered_list_item`, `divider`, and `table`. The first table under an H2 matching
/pricing/i becomes the interactive milestone checkboxes; columns are
`Milestone | Price | Timeline`, prices like `USD $2,000`. Unknown blocks fall back to
paragraphs. The cover and title block render from the row's properties, so the body
starts directly at Confidentiality.

## Section structure

1. **H1 Confidentiality Agreement** + one paragraph (boilerplate below).
2. **H1 Project Details**
   - H2 Background and Objectives: 2-3 paragraphs. Client's story in their own words,
     why now, what this proposal delivers. Mirror phrases from calls that resonated.
   - H2 Current Status: what exists today and its limits, stated respectfully.
   - H2 Goals and Aspirations: 3 bullets, each `Bold-ish lead: explanation`.
   - H2 Scope of Work: one framing paragraph, then H3 per milestone
     (`Milestone N: Name`) with deliverable bullets. State exclusions inline when they
     prevent scope creep (e.g. "a full brand guidelines document is not included at
     this stage").
   - H2 Technical Implementation (keep light when tech is secondary): H3 Platform,
     H3 Launch & Configuration, H3 Out of Scope (the consolidated exclusion list).
   - H2 Process Overview: "The work will follow an iterative and collaborative
     approach rather than a linear handoff model." + H3 per milestone explaining
     sequencing + "The general workflow will include:" bullets + the agreed schedule
     as dated bullets when one exists.
3. **H1 Project Agreement** (legals below, in this order)
4. **H1 Acceptance** + the acceptance sentence. Signature blocks are appended by the
   web/print renderer; do not write them into the body.

## Voice

Warm, direct, confident. Short sentences. No em dashes, ever. Concrete over abstract
("the survey feeds the mood boards" beats "inputs inform deliverables"). Sell the
outcome, respect the client's history ("the work has outgrown it" rather than "your
brand looks amateur").

## Legal boilerplate (verbatim)

**Confidentiality Agreement** (body, single paragraph)
> All the information contained in this document is the exclusive property of Joel Colombo (the Consultant) and provided to the Client for the sole purpose of evaluating this proposal. The Client agrees not to disclose, duplicate or use the information for any purpose other than assessing the proposal without prior written consent. Upon formalizing a contract, the Client will have the right to duplicate, use and disclose the material contained herein.

**H2 Collaboration Terms**
> In performing their respective obligations under this "Contract", the parties agree that their relationship is that of an independent contractor and not that of joint venturers, agents, employees, or part-time employees of the other party.

**H2 Pricing & Timelines** — the table, then a paragraph estimating the combined
timeline (reference the agreed schedule/launch date when one exists) ending with:
> Work will start as soon as the proposal is approved and the initial deposit is received.

**H2 Rates and Payment Terms**
> Payments will be structured as follows: 50% at project start, with the remaining balance invoiced upon completion of each agreed phase. If the project extends materially beyond the estimated timeline due to delayed feedback, paused approvals, or added scope, invoicing may be adjusted accordingly.
>
> Invoices are due NET 10. Work may be paused on overdue invoices. Balances overdue by more than 10 days may incur a 5% late fee.
>
> Any additional expenses, third-party licenses, premium assets, or externally contracted resources required by the Client will be billed separately with prior approval.
>
> Payments may be made via wire transfer, Wise, or another agreed payment method. Any payment processing fees are the sole responsibility of the Client.

**H2 Warranty**
> Following the completion of the project, a 30-day warranty period will be provided to address any unexpected issues that may arise after launch. Additional updates or new requests will be handled separately.

**H2 Ownership and Usage Rights**
> Upon full payment, the Client owns all rights to the approved work. The Consultant may display the work in portfolios and marketing materials, with appropriate credit to the Client. The Client warrants that it has the right to provide all content and will indemnify the Consultant against intellectual-property claims.
>
> The Consultant will take care to keep creative work distinctive but will not perform a trademark clearance search. It is the Client's responsibility to seek legal advice or conduct such searches to confirm originality and protectability of any marks.

**H2 Confidentiality**
> Both parties agree to keep all non-public information shared during the project confidential and use it only for project purposes. Confidential information includes business, marketing, design, or technical materials marked or understood to be confidential.
>
> Confidentiality does not apply to information already known, publicly available, lawfully obtained from third parties, independently developed, or required to be disclosed by law. Upon request, confidential materials must be returned, except for one archival copy retained for legal or compliance purposes.

**H2 Scheduling and Cancellation**
> Both parties commit to adhering to the agreed schedule. Canceling or rescheduling a task requires a minimum of 5 days' notice. Neither party is liable for delays due to force-majeure events.

**H2 Modification and Termination**
> This contract may be modified by mutual agreement with written notice. In the event of termination, all payments made up to that point are non-refundable, and the Client will remain responsible for all work completed and expenses incurred through the termination date. Termination for cause requires a 30-day notice unless the issue is remedied.

**H2 Errors or Omissions**
> The Client is responsible for proofreading and examining all deliverables. The Consultant cannot be held liable for errors discovered after final approval or third-party production.

**H2 Liability and Indemnification**
> The Consultant's liability is limited to the amount paid under this contract. Neither party is liable for indirect losses, including lost profits. The Client agrees to indemnify the Consultant against claims arising from the Client's use of the work, except where caused by the Consultant's gross negligence or willful misconduct. Each party must promptly notify the other of any such claims and cooperate in the defense.

**H2 Dispute Resolution**
> Disputes will first be resolved through mediation. If unresolved, disputes will be settled in a court located in the Province of Santa Fe, Argentina, under Argentinian law. Each party waives the right to a jury trial.

**H2 Entire Agreement**
> This document constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes prior discussions. If any provision is found unenforceable, it will be replaced with a valid provision that closely matches the parties' original intent.

**H1 Acceptance** (body)
> By signing below, the Client acknowledges acceptance of the terms, scope, timeline, and budget outlined in this proposal.
